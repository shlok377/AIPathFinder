Key					Action
C					Toggle Cinematic Mode (ON / OFF)
N					Toggle Smooth Panning (Cinematic Smoothing)
V					Toggle Black Bars (Cinematic Aspect Ratio)
"W, A, S, D"		Move camera (XZ Plane only)

Shift				Fly Down

Scroll Down / ↓		Decrease Speed & Sensitivity (-10%)
