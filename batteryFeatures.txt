1. Basic Consumption & Charging
   * Normal Drain: truck consumes certain % of battery while it is moving.
   * Passive Drain: A very slow drain even when idling to simulate standby power.
   * Automatic Charging: Battery refills when the truck is parked at any charging dock.


  2. Intelligent Logic (State Changes)
   * Low Power Mode: At a certain threshold (e.g., 20%), the truck refuses to pick up new tasks.
   * Emergency Return: If the battery is too low to complete a task, the truck forced-returns to the nearest dock (this situation should very rarely occur and should be avoided most of the times).


  3. Smart Task Assignment
   * Feasibility Check: Before assigning a task, the TaskSystem calculates if the truck has enough battery to reach the pickup, go to the drop-off, and then reach the nearest
     dock. If not, it looks for another truck.
